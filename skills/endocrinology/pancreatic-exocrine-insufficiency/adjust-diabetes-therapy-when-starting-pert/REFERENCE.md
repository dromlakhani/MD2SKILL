# REFERENCE: PERT and glycaemic monitoring

## Monitoring timeline (from Statements 4.2 and 5.4)
| When | What |
|---|---|
| Before PERT | Baseline HbA1c; regimen review; counselling |
| Weeks 0-4 | CGM (TIR, TAR, TBR, mean glucose, hypoglycaemic events); fasting and postprandial glucose; proactive titration |
| 2-4 weeks | Clinical response review (symptoms, weight, glycaemia) and PERT dose review |
| 3 months | Repeat HbA1c; SMBG as complement |
| Ongoing | Reassess glycaemic control at regular intervals |

## Adjustment direction (guideline)
- Hyperglycaemia: consider increasing mealtime insulin or adjusting pre-meal secretagogues.
- Hypoglycaemia: cautiously reduce insulin doses or discontinue sulfonylureas if enhanced insulin sensitivity is suspected.
- Coordinated endocrinology and gastroenterology care recommended.

## Evidence cited
- About 19% had mild hyperglycaemia after PERT start, some needing insulin escalation (Alexandre-Heymann 2024).
- PERT improved postprandial glucose (P<0.05) and HbA1c (P=0.003) in Mohan 1998 (tropical calculous pancreatitis).
- ADA general HbA1c benchmark <7% (53 mmol/mol), individualised.

## Not specified by the guideline
- CGM target ranges, SMBG frequency, insulin dose-change percentages, and specific thresholds for acting on TAR/TBR. Use local protocols.
