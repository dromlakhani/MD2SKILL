# REFERENCE

## Adult PERT dosing (lipase units)
| Setting | Dose |
|---|---|
| Main meal start | 25,000-50,000 |
| Snack | 10,000-25,000 |
| Empiric start (mild-moderate, outpatient) | 10,000 per meal |
| Escalation step | +25,000-50,000 per meal |
| Usual maximum | 75,000-100,000 per meal |
| >100,000 per meal | Exclude SIBO/coeliac/IBD; careful monitoring |

## Paediatric
- 1,000-4,000 units/kg/meal, not above adult maximum.
- Total max 10,000 units/kg/day.
- Under 4 years: about 1,000 units/kg starting; over 4 years: about 500 units/kg.
- Granules in acidic puree (pH <4.5); do not crush or chew.
- Monitor weight, height, BMI, growth velocity, puberty, nutritional markers.

## FE-1 interpretation
>200 normal; 100-200 mild-moderate; <100 severe.

## Vitamin supplementation (alongside PERT, if deficient)
- Vitamin D: 2000-4000 IU/day or more; target 25-OH-D at least 30 ng/mL.
- Vitamin E: 400-800 IU/day.
- Vitamin K: 5-10 mg weekly.
- Vitamin A: 10,000-25,000 IU/day, monitor serum retinol.
- Doses often 2-5x standard. Recheck every 6-12 months.

## PPI add-on
Omeprazole 20-40 mg or pantoprazole 40 mg daily, 30-60 min before the first meal; reassess at 2-4 weeks.

## Script candidates
- Paediatric dose per meal: weight_kg x 1,000 to 4,000 units, capped at the adult maximum.
- Paediatric daily cap: weight_kg x 10,000 units.
