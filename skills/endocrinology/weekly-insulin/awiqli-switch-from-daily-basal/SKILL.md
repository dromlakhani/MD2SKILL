# Switching from Daily Basal Insulin to Awiqli

The label uses a **one-time 50% extra dose in week 1** so icodec reaches steady state in 2–3 weeks instead of 3–4.

---

## Step 1 — Get the total daily basal dose (TDD-basal)

- Add up **all basal insulin** taken in a day (both doses if twice daily)
- Basal only — **do not include bolus/mealtime insulin**

---

## Step 2 — Time the first dose

**Give the first Awiqli dose on the day after the last dose of daily basal insulin.** That day becomes the weekly dosing day.

---

## Step 3 — Calculate the doses

| Week | Formula | Round to |
|---|---|---|
| **Week 1 (one time only)** | **TDD-basal × 7 × 1.5** | nearest 10 units |
| **Week 2** | **TDD-basal × 7** | nearest 10 units |
| **Week 3 onwards** | Titrate from the week 2 dose by fasting glucose, needs and goal | 10-unit steps |

**Worked examples (from the label's Table 1):**

| Daily basal (units) | Week 1 | Week 2 |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | 110 | 70 |
| 14 | 150 | 100 |
| 20 | 210 | 140 |
| 24 | 250 | 170 |
| 30 | 320 | 210 |
| 36 | 380 | 250 |
| 40 | 420 | 280 |
| 50 | 530 | 350 |
| 100 | 1,050 (split) | 700 |

**Doses above 700 units:** the pen delivers up to 700 units per injection — **split into two injections** (e.g. 1,050 = 700 + 350).

**Do not dial 700 units** unless 700 is the prescribed dose.

---

## Step 4 — Adjust everything else

- **Close glucose monitoring** during the switch, especially weeks 1–4
- **Bolus/mealtime insulin and other glucose-lowering drugs may need dose or timing changes**
- In the basal-bolus trial (Trial D), level 2 hypoglycaemia (<54 mg/dL) occurred in **50.9%** of icodec patients — the highest of all trials. Be ready to reduce mealtime insulin
- Consider reducing or stopping sulfonylureas/glinides
- Trial D outcome at 26 weeks: icodec weekly basal ~322 units vs glargine ~283 units, but **bolus insulin was lower** (~198 vs ~261 units/week)

---

## Step 5 — Train the patient (before the first dose)

- It is a **weekly** dose, not daily — the most important safety message
- Week 1 dose is **larger** than later doses — it's intentional and one-time
- The dosing day stays fixed each week
- Check the label and the dialled units before every injection
- Know how to recognise and treat hypoglycaemia

---

## Guardrails

- **Week 1 extra dose is one time only** — never repeat the 1.5× dose
- **Basal only** in the calculation — adding bolus doses causes a large overdose
- **Round to 10 units** — the pen can't dial in between
- **Split anything above 700 units**
- **Start the day after the last daily basal dose** — not the same day
- **Watch for hypoglycaemia on basal-bolus regimens** and cut bolus doses early if lows appear
- Don't use the weekly dose to handle short-term illness or diet change (see `awiqli-prescribing-guide`)
