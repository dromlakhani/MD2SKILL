# EXAMPLES

## 1. Child with T1DM
20 kg, age 8, FE-1 80 ug/g, steatorrhoea. Dose range per meal is 20,000-80,000 units, within the adult maximum. Total daily cap is 200,000 units (20 kg x 10,000). The guideline suggests about 500 units/kg initiation for over 4 years, i.e. about 10,000 per meal, then titrate. Use granules in applesauce if capsules cannot be swallowed. Monitor growth.

## 2. Refractory case
T1DM, 60 kg, on 75,000 units/meal, taken correctly, still has diarrhoea. Check adherence, then breath test for SIBO and coeliac serology, consider switching formulation, and add omeprazole 20-40 mg for 2-4 weeks. If more than 100,000 units/meal is needed, complete these exclusions first.

## 3. Glycaemic change after PERT
T2DM on insulin, 2 weeks after starting PERT, CGM shows rising post-meal TAR. Increase mealtime insulin. If instead nocturnal lows appear, reduce insulin cautiously.
